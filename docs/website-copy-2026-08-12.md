## Services

**Headline**  
Installation, repair, and scheduled service

**Lede**  
Tankless water heating is the work. Each quote is written from the job — equipment, venting, gas, water, and access. There is no published price list.

**Installation**  
We install Rinnai commercial and residential water heaters because of their high level of quality and durability. Typical jobs include replacing a storage tank, installing a first tankless unit, replacing an existing tankless, and new construction or remodel work coordinated with other trades.

**Repair**  
We diagnose and repair tankless systems of every make and model. Common parts and replacement units are on the truck. Most repairs are completed on the scheduled visit.

**Scheduled service**  
Flushing, inspection, and preventive maintenance, by appointment. Regular service protects efficiency and extends the life of the unit.

**Brands we service**  
New installations are specified as Rinnai. Repair and service are not limited to that brand.
