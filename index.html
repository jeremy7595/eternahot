Questions

Straight answers.

Do you come the same day?

We work by appointment. Call and tell us your issues. If we can help you reset or troubleshoot over the phone, we will.

How long does an install take?

Tank-to-tankless retrofits are one day installations. Unless it's a large scale commercial installation, most tankless installs are only 4 to 6 hours.

What do you need from me?

Address, photos of the current unit and the gas meter if you have them. The best photos are from far enough back that we can see and identify the piping attached to your current system.
What's important to us is the location of the water, venting, gas piping, and electricity, and the number of fixtures in the structure. Most of these questions can be answered over a short phone call.

Is this only Rinnai?

We specialize in Rinnai units for hot water production, but we do install other brands and components.

Do you do recirculation?

Yes. We specialize in recirculation systems. We like to use Grundfos recirculation pumps because of their reliability and high quality. We also troubleshoot and repair recirculation systems in residential and commercial settings.

Why is there no price on the form?

Every home is built in different locations, with different dimensions and characteristics. Pricing can be different depending on the size of the system, the type of recirculation or venting, or the equipment needed.

What about the Rinnai warranty?

Rinnai has a limited parts and labor warranty, and we are happy to work that system if you don't mind the wait. The factory program is not built for emergency service. Replacement parts can take days to arrive.

If you want the unit running today, we usually have the common parts in stock or close by. There is a reasonable markup on those parts. Warranty work also means a return trip, so there is a second labor charge when the part shows up.

On some jobs the wait is worth it — a heat exchanger is a good example. On a gas valve or a fan, it is usually better to buy the part at retail and finish the repair in one trip.

Do you pull permits?

Installations and major repairs will always be permitted and inspected.
