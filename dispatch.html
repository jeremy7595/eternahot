What $275 covers

The drive to your property, the fuel, and up to one hour on site. That hour is for diagnosis, or for gathering the information an estimate needs.

Credit toward a repair or replacement

If we offer a repair or a replacement, this $275 may be credited toward that work. The offer expires when the technician leaves the jobsite. The credit saves the fuel and the drive time of a return trip.

If parts must be ordered or picked up, a signed contract and a deposit lock the credit in. The $275 is not applied to the deposit. It is credited to the repair or replacement when that work is paid in full.
